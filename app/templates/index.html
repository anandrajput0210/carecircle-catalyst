Collaborative Elderly Care & Medication Management

CareCircle connects elders, primary caregivers, and family members in a unified care network. With atomic duplicate-confirmation protection, elder-friendly accessibility, and Gemini-powered adherence insights.

ⓘ Operational Reality: Just-In-Time Escalation

Under the free-tier serverless architecture (Vercel Hobby & Firebase Spark), missed-dose escalation operates via Just-In-Time (JIT) evaluation triggered during dashboard visits, client polling, or scheduled sync pings. Escalation does not guarantee real-time push alerts when no member is actively using the application or when external schedulers are inactive.

Core Architecture Pillars

👤

Elder Independence

High-contrast, large-touch interfaces designed specifically for senior cognitive ease. One-tap dose logging with clear color and shape pill recognition.

🛡

Duplicate-Dose Lock

Atomic Firestore transactions prevent dangerous double-dosing. If an elder or caregiver confirms a dose, any simultaneous confirmation is immediately blocked.

👪

Caregiver & Family Roles

Caregivers maintain prescription schedules and handle escalations. Family members stay informed with reassuring, read-only adherence activity timelines.

✨

Gemini Adherence Insights

Pattern-based adherence analysis that discovers behavioral trends (such as weekend schedule drift) with strict, hardcoded medical safety disclaimers.
